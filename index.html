Who you're working with

One engineer, the whole stack.

I'm Nicolas Quiroz. I build and run the technology small businesses depend on — the website customers land on, the internet line and network behind the counter, the server in the closet, and the backups that mean a bad day doesn't become a lost year.

Most small businesses end up with three or four vendors who each own a piece and none of whom own the result. The web company blames the host, the host blames the network, and the person who wired it left two years ago. Nobody has the whole picture, so the same problem keeps coming back.

The approach is deliberately unglamorous: find the real constraint first, fix that, and write down what was done. Every account, licence and domain is registered in your name — if you ever want to leave, you take the whole system with you.

A business of roughly 3–100 people with no IT department — an office, a clinic, a shop, a trades company or a growing startup. You need it to work, not a project plan about working.
