How account credit works

Credit is held in dollars, not in points or units — $1 paid is $1 of credit. There's nothing to redeem and nothing that expires.

When I invoice you, your balance is applied to that invoice automatically and only the shortfall is payable. Every invoice shows the credit applied and what's left, so the running balance is always on paper. If we part ways with credit unused, it's refunded — it's your money on deposit, not a store voucher.

Questions about an invoice

Email billing@nsqrai.com with your invoice number and I'll respond within one business day.

Payment terms, scope and any agreed milestones are stated on the invoice itself. If something doesn't match what we discussed, tell me before paying — it's far easier to correct an invoice than to reverse a payment.
